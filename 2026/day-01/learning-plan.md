Day 01 – Introduction to DevOps and Cloud

1. What is your understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering?

DevOps is a way of working where developers and operations teams work together.
It helps build, test and release software faster and with fewer mistakes.
The focus is on teamwork, automation, and continuous improvement.

Cloud means using the internet to store data and run applications.
Instead of keeping everything on your own computer or server, you use online services.
It helps you access your files and apps anytime, from anywhere.
